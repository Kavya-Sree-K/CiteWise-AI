Employee Onboarding SOP (United States) v2

Purpose

This procedure describes what HR, IT and managers do before and during a new hire's first weeks.

Scope

This document applies to employees based in the United States. It is owned by Angie Henderson and is effective from 2024-09-10.

Rules

HR sends the offer pack at least 5 working days before the start date.

IT prepares the laptop and accounts before the new hire's first day.

Every new hire completes the mandatory security and compliance training within 30 days of joining.

Managers hold a check-in conversation with the new hire at the end of weeks one, four and twelve.

Procedure

1. HR confirms the start date and creates the employee record.

2. IT creates the accounts and orders the laptop.

3. The manager prepares a 30-60-90 day plan.

4. The new hire completes orientation and mandatory training.

5. HR closes the onboarding case after the twelve-week check-in.

Escalation

Delays in equipment or access are escalated to the IT service desk by the manager.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to hr-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under Employee Onboarding SOP (United States) (version 2), the standard limit is 365 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.
