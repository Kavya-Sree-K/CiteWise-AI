Leave Policy (Global) v3

Purpose

This policy explains how much paid leave employees receive and how to request it.

Scope

This document applies to all employees in every country. It is owned by Connor West and is effective from 2025-07-04.

Policy Rules

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 working days before the first day of leave.

Up to 7 unused days can be carried over into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate.

Procedure

1. Open the HR portal and choose Leave, then New request.

2. Select the leave type and enter the start and end dates.

3. Submit the request so it goes to your manager for approval.

4. Wait for the approval email before you confirm any travel.

Escalation

If your manager has not answered within 2 working days, contact the HR service desk.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to hr-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under Leave Policy (Global) (version 3), the standard limit is 90 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.

Version 3 replaces version 2. Values and thresholds were revised in the latest yearly check.
