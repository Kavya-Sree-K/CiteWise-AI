Security Incident Reporting SOP (Global) v1

Purpose

This procedure explains how employees report a suspected security incident.

Scope

This document applies to all employees in every country. It is owned by Cameron Parker and is effective from 2024-12-30.

Rules

Any employee who notices a suspected security incident must report it within 4 hours of noticing it.

The security desk can be reached on extension 5520 at any time of the day.

Employees must not try to investigate or fix the incident on their own.

Reporting in good faith never leads to disciplinary action, even if the alarm turns out to be false.

Procedure

1. Stop using the affected device and do not switch it off.

2. Call the security desk on extension 5520 within 4 hours of noticing the problem.

3. Write down what you saw, the time, and any error messages.

4. Send the written report to the security team by email.

5. Wait for the security team to confirm that the case is open.

Escalation

Cases that involve customer data are escalated to the security manager within 2 hours.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to security-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under Security Incident Reporting SOP (Global) (version 1), the standard limit is 365 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.
