VPN Access SOP (Sales) v1

Purpose

This procedure explains how to request, set up and use the company VPN.

Scope

This document applies to everyone working in the Sales division. It is owned by Gabrielle Davis and is effective from 2024-07-18.

Rules

VPN access is granted on request and needs manager approval.

Sessions are disconnected automatically after 60 minutes of inactivity.

Personal devices may only connect if they are enrolled in device management.

VPN access is reviewed every 180 days and removed if it was not used.

Procedure

1. Raise a VPN request in the IT service portal.

2. Wait for your manager to approve the request.

3. Install the VPN client from the company software centre.

4. Sign in with your company account and your authenticator code.

Escalation

Connection problems that last more than one hour are escalated to the network team.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to it-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under VPN Access SOP (Sales) (version 1), the standard limit is 365 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.
