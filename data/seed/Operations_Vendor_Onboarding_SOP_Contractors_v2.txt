Vendor Onboarding SOP (Contractors) v2

Purpose

This procedure describes how new vendors are checked and added to the approved vendor list.

Scope

This document applies to contractors and temporary staff. It is owned by Cathy Robinson and is effective from 2024-09-16.

Rules

Every new vendor must complete the due-diligence questionnaire.

Vendors that handle personal data need a security review before approval.

Onboarding is completed within 20 working days when all documents are provided.

Approved vendors are reviewed once every 6 months.

Procedure

1. The requester submits a vendor request in the procurement system.

2. The vendor completes the due-diligence questionnaire.

3. Procurement, legal and security complete their checks.

4. Procurement adds the vendor to the approved vendor list.

Escalation

Vendors that fail a check can only be approved by the chief operating officer.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to operations-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under Vendor Onboarding SOP (Contractors) (version 2), the standard limit is 90 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.

Version 2 replaces version 1. Values and thresholds were revised in the latest yearly check.
