Remote Work Policy (Singapore) v1

Purpose

This policy sets out when and how employees may work from home or another remote location.

Scope

This document applies to employees based in Singapore. It is owned by Jason Powell and is effective from 2025-01-05.

Policy Rules

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their manager's agreement.

Core collaboration hours are 09:30 to 15:30, and employees must be reachable during them.

Remote work must happen from a country where the company has a legal entity, for no more than 20 days per year.

A monthly home-office allowance of 40 USD is paid after the first month of remote work.

Procedure

1. Agree your remote working pattern with your manager.

2. Record the agreed pattern in the HR portal.

3. Confirm that your home network meets the minimum security standard.

4. Review the arrangement with your manager every six months.

Escalation

Disagreements about remote work patterns are escalated to the HR business partner.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to hr-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under Remote Work Policy (Singapore) (version 1), the standard limit is 270 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.
