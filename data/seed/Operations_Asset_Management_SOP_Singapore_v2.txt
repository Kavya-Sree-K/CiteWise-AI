Asset Management SOP (Singapore) v2

Purpose

This procedure explains how company equipment is recorded, tracked and retired.

Scope

This document applies to employees based in Singapore. It is owned by John Daniel and is effective from 2024-10-18.

Rules

Every asset above 300 USD is recorded in the asset register with an owner.

A physical asset check is performed every 12 months.

Employees who leave must return all assets on their last working day.

Retired assets are wiped and disposed of through an approved recycler.

Procedure

1. Tag the new asset and record it in the asset register.

2. Assign an owner and a location.

3. Update the register whenever the asset moves or changes owner.

4. Retire the asset and record the disposal certificate.

Escalation

Missing assets are reported to the security desk and to the finance controller.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to operations-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under Asset Management SOP (Singapore) (version 2), the standard limit is 180 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.

Version 2 replaces version 1. Values and thresholds were revised in the latest yearly check.
