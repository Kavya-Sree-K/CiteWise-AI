Code of Conduct (India) v3

Purpose

This code describes the behaviour expected from everyone who works for the company.

Scope

This document applies to employees based in India. It is owned by Daniel Baker and is effective from 2025-08-09.

Policy Rules

Employees must treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect and must not harass or discriminate.

Gifts worth more than 50 USD must be declared to the compliance team within 5 days.

Conflicts of interest must be reported to your manager as soon as you become aware of them.

Concerns can be raised confidentially through the ethics hotline on extension 5601.

Procedure

1. Write down what happened, when and who was involved.

2. Raise the concern with your manager, HR or the ethics hotline.

3. HR acknowledges the concern within 5 working days.

4. HR investigates and informs you of the outcome.

Escalation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to hr-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under Code of Conduct (India) (version 3), the standard limit is 180 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.

Version 3 replaces version 2. Values and thresholds were revised in the latest yearly check.
