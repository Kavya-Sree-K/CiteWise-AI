Corporate Card Policy (Global) v2

Purpose

This policy controls how company credit cards are issued and used.

Scope

This document applies to all employees in every country. It is owned by Fred Smith and is effective from 2025-08-04.

Policy Rules

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel or buy on behalf of the company.

The monthly limit is 5,000 USD unless the finance controller approves a higher limit.

Cash withdrawals and personal purchases are not allowed.

Statements must be reconciled within 10 days of the statement date.

Procedure

1. Request a card in the finance portal with your manager's approval.

2. Activate the card and sign the cardholder agreement.

3. Upload receipts for every card transaction.

4. Reconcile the monthly statement before the deadline.

Escalation

A lost or stolen card must be reported to the bank and to finance on the same day.

Contacts

Send questions about this document by email to finance-help@company.local.

Document Control

Under Corporate Card Policy (Global) (version 2), the standard limit is 365 days. This is the maximum number of days between two checks of the document by its owner.

Version 2 replaces version 1. Values and thresholds were revised in the latest yearly check.
